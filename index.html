Tim Robbins is an independent arbitrator with nearly twenty years of experience in commercial and investor-state disputes, and has acted as arbitrator, tribunal secretary or counsel in over ninety international arbitrations. Tim provides a wide breadth of experience, knowledge and perspective from his work with tribunals, in private practice and at arbitral institutions.

Based in The Hague and Hong Kong, Tim is qualified as a solicitor in England & Wales, and called to the bars of New York and Ontario. He has acted as presiding, sole and co-arbitrator in arbitrations administered by the ICC, LCIA, HKIAC, SIAC, SCC, DIAC and KCAB, in addition to working on matters under the rules of other leading institutions, including ICSID, ICDR and JCAA, as well as numerous ad hoc proceedings under the UNCITRAL Rules.

Tim has a particular focus on efficiency in arbitration, and has written and spoken extensively on the subject. If you are interested in copies of any publications or would like to arrange a seminar or presentation, please get in touch.
